The issue's mock (#838) with three changes: the class names are the game's own (MAGIC-USER, not MAGE), so the NEEDS
column moves from 14 to 15; HAS gives the scores alone, in the order NEEDS names them; and every class a human may take
is listed (the PHB's prime requisites: a paladin's are Strength and Wisdom, a ranger's Strength, Intelligence and
Wisdom). A class qualifies when the character has 17 in each of its prime requisites, its minimums and an alignment it
allows, and the hall trains it. With none qualifying the menu is EXIT alone and the explanation says why.
