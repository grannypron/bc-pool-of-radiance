# Alt-1 to Alt-8 open a party member's items (#1175), from save G in the central wilderness (saves/wild): Alt-2 opens
# the second member's items and Escape puts the wilderness back, Alt-7 with six members does nothing, F1 lists the
# keys, and Alt-1 in camp opens the first member's items. A golden build without #1175 does nothing on Alt, so its
# items checkpoints differ by design.
boot NORAND
ignore POOL OF RADIANCE V
wait RADIANCE
put saves/wild
press l g
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 60
press alt-2
wait TRADE 20
checkpoint items
press esc
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
checkpoint items-closed
press alt-7
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
checkpoint no-member-7
press f1
wait HELP 20
checkpoint keys
press esc
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
press e
wait CAMP 60
press alt-1
wait TRADE 20
checkpoint camp-items
press esc
wait CAMP 20
checkpoint camp-closed
