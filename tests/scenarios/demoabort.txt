# The demo (#1109) ended by a key, mid-walk and mid-battle: the main menu left alone for thirty seconds with no party
# plays it, Space during the walk ends it, the main menu waits another thirty seconds and plays it again, and Space in
# the battle ends that one. Each time the main menu comes back with no party; the saves are untouched; and a character
# created then rolls what chargen's does (its rolled checkpoint), so the demo left the random numbers where they were,
# and save A loads. The System page's free memory (ignored in the comparison: check its lines in obj/regress.json) is
# about 11.5 KB lower after the demo than before, and stays there however often it plays: what the game keeps loaded
# until something replaces it, not a leak (#1226). Measured with STING Ctrl-Y (EGA, 1 MB) and a build that logged
# MemAvail and Blit.picBytes after each script command: a walk ended by a key keeps 12,407 bytes of pictures, Podol
# Plaza's wall sets (LOAD PIECES, 8,481) and the title portrait (EclMenu.DisplayPortrait, 3,926); a battle ended by a
# key keeps the wall sets and the four statistics blocks of the demo's party (Stats.NewEntry, 1,016 bytes each), made at
# a member's first counted blow and kept for the next party's members, as their first fight would make them. Loading
# save A afterwards leaves the same pictures as loading it without the demo, and the blocks wait for its first fight.
# Needs DEMO.DAX, which regress.sh stages for a scenario named demo*; a golden build without the demo fails at the
# first wait.
boot NORAND
ignore POOL OF RADIANCE V
ignore FREE MEMORY|LARGEST BLOCK|COMMIT  |VERSION  
strip V\d+ +MERGED
wait RADIANCE
press g y
checkpoint memory-before
press esc
wait RADIANCE
saves before
wait-text GET.READY.FOR 60
press spc
wait RADIANCE 30
checkpoint walk-ended
wait-text HITPOINTS 120
force spc
wait RADIANCE 60
checkpoint battle-ended
saves after
press g y
checkpoint memory-after
press esc
wait RADIANCE
press c
checkpoint races
press end end end end end ret ret end end end end ret ret
checkpoint rolled
press y a r n o l d ret k y k y
wait RADIANCE
press l
press a
