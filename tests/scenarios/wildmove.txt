# The wilderness menu's Move (#1159), from save G in the central wilderness at 27,27 (saves/wild): Move turns the
# bottom line to Exit; Page Up steps north-east and Home north-west, each 18 hours, and the Right arrow east, 12 hours;
# Escape gives the menu back, where Home picks the previous character again. A golden build has no Move, so it fails
# until the goldens are recorded from a build with it.
boot NORAND
ignore POOL OF RADIANCE V
wait RADIANCE
put saves/wild
press l g
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 60
checkpoint menu
press m
wait ^EXIT 20
checkpoint move
press pgup
wait ^EXIT 60
checkpoint northeast
press home
wait ^EXIT 60
checkpoint northwest
press right
wait ^EXIT 60
checkpoint east
press esc
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
checkpoint menu-again
press home
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
checkpoint previous-character
