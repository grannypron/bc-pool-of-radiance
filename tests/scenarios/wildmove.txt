# The wilderness menu's Move (#1159) and its wide view (#1186), from save G in the central wilderness at 27,27
# (saves/wild). Move turns the bottom line to the status and Exit and the top of the screen to one map 13 tiles across,
# the party list gone. A step west reaches Phlan's eastern edge, whose script narrows the view for its picture and
# menu, the party list back; LEAVE, and the menu widens the view again. Page Up steps north-east and Home north-west,
# each 18 hours, and the Right arrow east, 12 hours; F1's keys and Alt-2's items come back to the wide view; Escape
# gives the menu and the 5x5 view back, where Home picks the previous character again. A golden build has no Move, so
# it fails until the goldens are recorded from a build with it.
boot NORAND
ignore POOL OF RADIANCE V
wait RADIANCE
put saves/wild
press l g
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 60
checkpoint menu
press m
wait EXIT$ 20
checkpoint move
press left
wait LEAVE 60
checkpoint phlan
press l
wait EXIT$ 60
checkpoint phlan-left
press pgup
wait EXIT$ 60
checkpoint northeast
press home
wait EXIT$ 60
checkpoint northwest
press right
wait EXIT$ 60
checkpoint east
press f1
wait HELP 20
press esc
wait EXIT$ 20
checkpoint keys-closed
press alt-2
wait TRADE 20
press esc
wait EXIT$ 20
checkpoint items-closed
press esc
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
checkpoint menu-again
press home
wait ^(MOVE )?CAST VIEW ENCAMP 20
checkpoint previous-character
